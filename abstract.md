Finite ranked systems represent regular trees through unfold-equivalence.
Set-systems add nondeterministic successors and root vertices; their yields
give rise to ordered set-algebras and a yield-syntactic quotient.
The central algebraic statement connects recognition by rankwise-finite
algebras with recognition by unfold-automata. For finite reachable
transition systems, the main logical statement identifies the
bisimulation-invariant fragment of monadic second-order logic with the
modal $\mu$-calculus.
