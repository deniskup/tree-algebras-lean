This submission formalizes Theorem 2 of
[arXiv:2407.12677v7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12677v7):
over a finite proposition alphabet, the bisimulation-invariant
fragment of monadic second-order logic on finite reachable transition systems
is exactly the modal $\mu$-calculus. Both implications and their supporting
proof network are proved. The algebraic construction uses finite ranked
systems, yield-syntactic set-algebras, and unfold-automata.

The submission also provides a total executable MSO-to-$\mu$ translation
with correctness under the finite bisimulation-invariance promise.
Explicit algebra, profile, word-automaton and finite-model bounds justify
its exhaustive finite search.
